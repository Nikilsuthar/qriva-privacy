Privacy Policy

App: QRiva - QR Scanner, Creator & Label Printer (package com.nikhilsuthar.qriva)
Last updated: 20 September 2026

This policy explains what information the QRiva app ("the app") uses and how. The app is published by Nikhil D Suthar ("we", "us"). You can reach us at nikhilsuthar301@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no servers of ours: what you scan and create stays on your phone.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which uses some device data (see "Advertising").
  • Camera, photos and location are used only when you ask the app to (scan, pick an image, "Use my current location").

Information the app handles on your device

  • QR codes you create and scan, and your history are stored only on your device (app storage). We do not receive them. You can delete them any time from History or Settings → Clear history, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Camera: used only while the scanner is open, to read QR codes. Camera images are processed on your device (Google ML Kit's on-device barcode model), and are not saved or sent by us.
  • Photos / images: only an image you choose yourself (to scan a code from a picture or to use as a logo in a QR code) is read. It stays on your device.
  • Saving and sharing: when you save a QR image it is written to your gallery ("QRiva" album). When you share or print, the image goes to the app or printer you choose.
  • Location (optional): only if you tap Use my current location in the Location QR form, and only after you allow the permission. The app reads your position once to fill in the address or coordinates. To turn the position into a street address, the phone's system geocoding service (which may be provided by Google) may receive those coordinates. We do not store your location or send it to our own servers, and the app never tracks you in the background. You can also type an address instead and never grant this permission.
  • Region hint: to hide options for services that are unavailable in your country (for example TikTok in India), the app reads your device's SIM/network country, language region and time zone locally. This is not stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

QRiva is free and shows banner and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use identifiers such as your Advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), app interaction data and diagnostics to serve, measure and secure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can review or change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy options.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Google's own policy is at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with third parties, other than the advertising provider described above and the system services you choose to use (share sheet, printer, maps). Links you open from the app (websites, social profiles, maps) are handled by the corresponding app or browser and are governed by their own policies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security and retention

We keep no user data on our own servers. Data stored on your device is under your control and is removed when you clear the history or uninstall the app. Ads are loaded over encrypted connections.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete history at any time in the app; deny or revoke camera/location permission in Android settings.
  • Change ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy options (where shown) or Android ad settings.
  • Because we do not hold your data, requests to access or delete data held by advertising partners should go to Google.

Third-party names

QRiva is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat, X, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Telegram or Google. All names and logos belong to their owners and are used only to describe the type of link.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's update notes.
